VeEv Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 11, 2026

Developer: Wayne Herrington

Contact: wayneherrington88@gmail.com

VeEv is an AI companion app. This policy explains, in plain language, what happens to your information when you use it.

The short version

Your conversations and VeEv's memory of you are stored on your device, not on our servers. We do not run servers that receive your chats. We do not sell your data. We do not show ads. We do not use analytics or tracking tools. No account is required to use VeEv.

What is stored on your device

The following stays on your phone or tablet, in the app's private storage:

  • Your conversations with VeEv.
  • VeEv's memory: notes she keeps so she can learn your preferences and continue your conversations.
  • Your settings, including her appearance options.
  • The API key for the AI service you choose to connect (see below), if you add one.
  • Files you attach (photos, videos, documents) when they are part of a conversation.

Uninstalling VeEv deletes all of this. You can also clear conversations and memory from inside the app at any time.

The AI service you connect

VeEv does not include its own AI account for you. To chat, you connect an AI service of your choice using your own API key.

When you send a message, VeEv sends your message — and the conversation context and any attachment you include with it — directly from your device to the AI service you connected, so it can generate a reply. That transmission is encrypted (HTTPS/TLS).

We do not receive a copy. What that AI service does with the data you send it is governed by that service's own privacy policy, which you should read before connecting it. Your API key is stored only on your device and is used only to make those requests.

Attachments

You choose what to attach: camera photos, pictures, videos, or files from your device. Attachments stay on your device unless you include them in a message you send, in which case they are transmitted to your connected AI service as described above.

Voice

If you use voice input, your speech is handled by your device's built-in speech services. Depending on your device and settings, those services may process your voice on the device or on their own servers under their own policies. VeEv does not record or keep audio itself. Read replies aloud is off by default; if you turn it on, your device's text-to-speech voice speaks the replies on your device.

Permissions VeEv uses

  • Microphone — only if you use voice input.
  • Camera — only if you choose to take a photo to attach.
  • Photos, videos, and files — only the items you pick, when you pick them.
  • Internet — to reach the AI service you connected, and to open links you tap.

VeEv asks for each permission only when you use the feature that needs it.

What we never do

  • We never sell or rent your personal data.
  • We never show ads.
  • We never use analytics, advertising IDs, or tracking SDKs.
  • We never collect your conversations, memory, attachments, or API key on our own servers — we don't have servers that could receive them.

Children

VeEv is not directed at children under 13, and it is not designed for them. The app offers open-ended conversation with an AI service, which is intended for older users. If you believe a child under 13 has used VeEv, contact us and we will help you remove the app's local data (all of it is on the device and is removed by uninstalling).

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and change the effective date above. Material changes will also be noted in the app's release notes on Google Play.
