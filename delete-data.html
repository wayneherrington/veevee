VeEv — Delete Your Data

App: VeEv

Developer: Wayne Herrington

Contact: wayneherrington88@gmail.com

VeEv stores your data only on your own device. The developer runs no servers and keeps no copy of your data, so there is nothing on our side to delete. You can delete everything yourself, at any time, in either of these ways:

Option 1 — Clear data inside the app

  1. Open VeEv.
  2. Open Settings.
  3. Use the clear option to erase your conversations and VeEv's memory of you.

Option 2 — Uninstall VeEv (deletes everything)

  1. Open your device's Settings, then Apps, then VeEv.
  2. Tap Uninstall.
  3. Uninstalling permanently deletes all of VeEv's data from your device.

What data is deleted

Both options delete all of the following, completely and immediately:

  • Your conversations with VeEv.
  • VeEv's memory: the notes she keeps to learn your preferences.
  • Your settings and appearance choices.
  • The API key for the AI service you connected, if you added one.
  • Files you attached that are stored with your conversations.

What data is kept, and for how long

None. VeEv's developer holds no copy of your data — not during use, and not after deletion. There is no retention period because there is nothing retained. Deletion on your device is final.

One exception that is outside VeEv: messages you sent were delivered to the AI service you chose and connected, so that it could reply. That service's handling of data you sent it is governed by that service's own privacy policy. If you want data removed there, contact that AI service provider.

If you cannot access the app and need guidance, contact us at wayneherrington88@gmail.com and we will walk you through the steps above. Because all data lives only on your device, deletion is always completed by clearing the app or uninstalling it, as described on this page.
