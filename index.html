Just ask — the way you’d say it to a person

There are no magic words and no exact phrases to memorize. Say it however it comes out — any wording works, at any time. The old short phrases still work too, they’re just one way among many.

  • Tell VV something to keep in mind — just tell her and ask her to hold onto it, however you’d naturally say it, like telling a friend about working nights or an appointment coming up
  • Introduce yourself — just tell VV your name in the middle of talking, the way you would with a person
  • Share how your heart is doing — mention a number out of ten in any sentence, or just describe how you feel in words
  • Ask for prayer or a verse — ask any way that feels natural when you need prayer, comfort, or something from Scripture
  • Ask what VV is holding for you — ask in your own words what she remembers from this talk
  • Talk in your language — speak or type in yours; VV will tell you the English meaning and answer you back in your language. Ask how to say something in another language any way you’d ask a friend
  • Ask VV anything — math she works out for real, money, sports, general knowledge, life. If it’s about a fact or an event, she researches it herself first — Wikipedia plus her own browser — and tells you where it came from
  • Bet math and picks — mention odds, a stake, a matchup, or just ask who wins however you’d ask a person; she’ll run the numbers or call it with reasons and honest confidence
  • When you’re hurting — just say how you feel, in your own words; VV answers like a friend and will tell you straight what she thinks you need to do

Put Vee Vee on your phone — Android & iPhone

This page is the app. Put it on your home screen and it opens like any other app — no store, no terminal, nothing to paste.

📱 Android

  1. Open this page in Chrome.
  2. Tap the ⋮ menu (top right).
  3. Tap Add to Home screen / Install app.
  4. Tap Add — Vee Vee is now on your phone.

🍎 iPhone

  1. Open this page in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button (square with an arrow).
  3. Tap Add to Home Screen.
  4. Tap Add — Vee Vee is now on your phone.

Once VV is on your home screen, tapping the star opens VV full-screen, like a real app — on Android and iPhone both. Anyone you send this page's link to can do the same steps on their own phone.
